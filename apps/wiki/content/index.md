Quorum is an incremental systems game. You are the first engineer of a startup. You wire
Components on a board, and requests flow through your Design. Each fix moves the bottleneck
somewhere else.

This wiki explains the rules behind that. Every number on it comes from the game's own catalog,
so it cannot disagree with the game. When the game changes, the wiki changes with it.

## Where to start

- New to Quorum? Read [Your first company](/start/first-company).
- Stuck on a slow or failing Design? Read [Find the bottleneck](/guides/find-the-bottleneck).
- Want the numbers? Open [Components](/components) or the [Research tree](/research).
- Want to know why it works this way? Read [How requests flow](/mechanics/requests).

## Spoilers

Each page notes the Era where it matters. Pick the last Era you reached in the toolbar, and
pages about later Eras fold until you open them.
